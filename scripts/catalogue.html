About us

JM Masala Trading LLP

JM Masala Trading LLP is a spice and seed supplier based at the APMC Market Yard in Unjha, Gujarat. We supply spices and seeds for export and bulk wholesale, with cumin as our flagship export.

Supply terms

  • Trial orders from 1 MT
  • Supply up to 200 MT per month
  • Dispatch 1-5 days after order confirmation
  • Samples can be arranged
  • Private label available

Packing

  • 25 kg or 50 kg PP bags with inner liner
  • Retail packs on request
  • Private-label packaging on request

Specifications, grades and packing for each product are confirmed with your quotation. Tell us the product, quantity, destination port and packing you need.

How to order

  1. Send your enquiry: product, quantity, destination country and packing preference.
  2. We reply with the specification and a quotation.
  3. A sample can be arranged for your approval.
  4. Confirm the order; dispatch follows within 1-5 days of confirmation.
